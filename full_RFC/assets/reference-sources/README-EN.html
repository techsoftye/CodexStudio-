Source and Translation Distinctions

Drafts one and two belong to the other agent. My earlier Arabic translation of draft two is not an independently authored English article. My first Arabic review introduced editorial additions, so it is separated from draft one's faithful translation to avoid conflating sources.

New translations are identified on their reading pages: the faithful Arabic translation of draft one, the English counterpart of the earlier edited Arabic review, English versions of the two commands, and the reference document's English translation. They are not new article revisions or silent changes to historical drafts.

Latest Decisions Before Reusing Commands

The approved name is TechSoft. The 900–1,200-word limit was removed. The priority is a balanced explanation of every tab with its images, without filler. Read 03-Prompts/00-Latest-Editorial-Decisions-EN.md before reusing older prompts.

YemenCoder and word-count constraints remain in historical originals to preserve the record, not to reverse the user's later decisions. Images retain their original visible text and have not been redesigned or edited.

Images, References, and Limits

Original text files have not been rewritten to repair image links. HTML previews resolve historical filenames and placeholders to the six local mockups; the Markdown originals keep their earlier publishing syntax and paths.

Some old review notes say images were unavailable during that review. These are preserved historical statements; the images are now included. No new external verification of product claims or references was conducted during packaging, and differences between drafts were not silently reconciled.

No complete Full RFC was written for this task. The archive contains the concept reference, outline, and instructions for preparing a detailed outline. Nothing was published to the forum or GitHub and no messages were sent.

Unchanged Originals and Duplicate Uploads

06-Original-Sources/source-manifest.json records original filenames and hashes. Byte-identical uploads with suffixes such as (1) and (2)(1), and duplicate image uploads, are not repeated.
