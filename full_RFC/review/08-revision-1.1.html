Revision 1.1 — Project Groups, hybrid teams and technology horizon

Date: 2026-10-18. Status: complete. Both editions assembled; 69 requirements and 617 local links checked; zero document errors. ZIP integrity verified.

What changed / ما تغير

The full concept now defines a product umbrella over independent projects, a versioned environment profile per execution target, dependency/contract links, integrated acceptance evidence, and a hybrid human/agent role matrix. The MetaStore example uses Backend, Flutter and Next.js as supplied by the user; it is illustrative, not a repository audit.

أصبح التصور يحدد مظلة منتج فوق مشاريع مستقلة، وملفات تعريف بيئات ذات إصدارات، وروابط اعتماد وعقود، وأدلة قبول مدمجة، ومصفوفة أدوار للبشر والوكلاء. يستخدم مثال MetaStore وصف المستخدم لـBackend وFlutter وNext.js؛ وهو توضيحي وليس تدقيق مستودعات.

Evidence and conclusions

E-14–E-30 add seventeen official or primary project/research sources; E-02 is refreshed. Current Codex, VS Code and GitLab capabilities are credited before stating the proposed extension. The comparison establishes existing foundations and a plausible coordination need; it does not establish measured demand for Studio or a promise that current account integrations expose every required control.

Review corrections

  • Generalized record ownership only for explicit group-owned coordination records; Tasks and Attempts remain Project-owned.
  • Preserved the no-Assignee creation form by placing responsibility in a separate task-detail action.
  • Distinguished local MVP from post-MVP normative requirements in the generated coverage ledger.
  • Kept provider/runtime policy separate from Studio roles; no group membership or agent naming grants access.
  • Required a shared authoritative service before team editing; no shared-database-copy shortcut.
  • Separated simulated, physical and provider evidence; no claimed quantum advantage.
  • Retained the original six PNGs and all original archive files; old ZIP is not overwritten.

Remaining external decisions

Q-01–Q-12 remain decision gates. The newly explicit Q-10–Q-12 concern group/contract ownership, shared identity/enforcement, and modality/domain policies. Product behavior is specified for review; organization-specific role bindings and provider guarantees need approval and feasibility evidence before implementation.

No product code, MetaStore repositories, real credentials, devices or provider jobs were executed or modified. No publication, external message or GitHub upload occurred.
