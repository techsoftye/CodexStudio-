    Latest Editorial Decisions — Read Before Reusing Earlier Commands

    File type: A summary of later user decisions in the conversation, prepared to organize this archive. It is not a verbatim earlier command and does not provide new authorization to execute or publish anything.

    1. The approved article and outreach identity is TechSoft. YemenCoder in historical drafts and commands is preserved as part of their history, not the approved name for new publication.
    2. The 900–1,200-word constraint was removed. Do not sacrifice important explanation to fit a number. The article must still be balanced, engaging, and free of repetition and filler.
    3. Opening: Why not offer an integrated environment for developer work, when many underlying capabilities already exist but their experience or connections feel scattered in practical use? Do not turn a personal observation into an established technical claim.
    4. Explain every tab separately: Chat, Tasks, Decisions, Results, Library, and More, including its functions, actions, right panel, and relationships to the rest of the project. Do not reduce them to a short list of definitions.
    5. Place relevant mockups with their sections. Tasks includes both the task-list/stages/sections view and the new-task form. No standalone More image exists among the six unique images; do not invent one.
    6. Ground the article in firsthand experience: Use a professional technical voice without invented results, numbers, or claims of official endorsement.
    7. Principal direction: Codex Studio as a dedicated developer desktop product reusing Codex capabilities. Discuss independence and integration trade-offs without representing them as settled facts.
    8. Agreed deliverables: An English article, an Arabic review version, numbered images, matching image placeholders, and a ZIP package.
    9. Full RFC details: Keep them separate from the community post. Make them available later when complete, with interested readers invited to request them privately. No invented GitHub link or claim that an unfinished document is ready.
    10. Keep the phases separate: Phase One is the article; Phase Two is the detailed RFC outline only. Writing all chapters, publishing, or uploading files needs separate instruction.

    Earlier commands are preserved in their original form for review. Read these decisions alongside them before resuming work, especially the authorship change and removal of the word-count constraint.
